## Polyhedral Template Matching (PTM)

The PTM template, convex-hull graph, automorphism, quaternion and remapping tables in
`crates/rmda-core/src/analysis/ptm/` (`tables.rs`, `quat_tables.rs`, `remap_tables.rs`,
`refdata.rs`) are transcribed from the PTM reference implementation
(<https://github.com/pmla/polyhedral-template-matching>), regenerated by the scripts in `tools/`.
The algorithm is P. M. Larsen, S. Schmidt, J. Schiøtz, *Robust structural identification via
polyhedral template matching*, Modelling Simul. Mater. Sci. Eng. 24, 055007 (2016).

## OVITO

Several analyses follow OVITO 3.15's algorithms step for step so that their results agree with
OVITO atom for atom: common-neighbour analysis and its diamond variant, PTM's Voronoi neighbour
order, the automatic grain segmentation (`GrainSegmentationModifier`, `GraphClusteringAuto`),
Wigner–Seitz occupancy, cluster analysis and the RDF conventions. The modules say which OVITO
routine they follow. OVITO (<https://www.ovito.org>) is by OVITO GmbH and A. Stukowski; its open
source code is available under the MIT licence (and GPLv3), whose notice above applies with
"Copyright (c) OVITO GmbH, Germany". A. Stukowski, *Visualization and analysis of atomistic
simulation data with OVITO – the Open Visualization Tool*, Modelling Simul. Mater. Sci. Eng. 18,
015012 (2010).

Test expectations in `tests/data/` (`*ovito*`, `*_expected*`) were produced by running OVITO on the
fixtures there.
